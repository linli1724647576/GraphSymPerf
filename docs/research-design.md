# Research design

The primary contribution is certificate-carrying graph-state symmetry plus a runtime-first oracle. GDsmith supplies the base property graph; GraphSymPerf materializes two disconnected, involutively isomorphic copies modulo the reserved replay `id`, then instantiates only graph-pattern endpoint, fixed-path endpoint, and incident-pattern-order symmetries from observed topology.

Boolean redundancy and general-purpose equivalent-query rewriting are outside the formal path. Root-cause labels are post-hoc explanation and deduplication aids, never generation targets. See [the current implementation method outline](../doc/method-outline-current-implementation.md).
